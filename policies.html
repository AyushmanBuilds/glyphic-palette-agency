All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We may change prices or withdraw an e-book at any time; the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

We collect only what we need to complete your order and support you.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile number, entered at checkout.
  • Order details: which e-books you bought and when.
  • Basic site analytics such as pages visited and device type.

Payment details

Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure page. We never see or store them.

How we use your data

To deliver your e-book, send order and support messages, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the shop, such as our payment processor and email provider.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing info@glyphicpalette.com. We keep order records for as long as the law requires.

Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

E-books are digital products delivered immediately, so orders cannot be cancelled once the download link has been sent, and we do not offer refunds for change of mind.

When we will refund you

  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Payment was taken but you did not receive your e-book and we cannot deliver it.
  • The file is corrupt or will not open and we cannot fix it.
  • You received a different e-book from the one you ordered.

How to ask

Email info@glyphicpalette.com within 7 days of purchase with your order reference and the email used at checkout. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Digital delivery policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

We do not ship physical goods. After your payment is confirmed, a download link for your e-book is sent to the email address you entered at checkout, usually within a few minutes.

  • If you do not see the email, check your spam or promotions folder.
  • Make sure you entered your email address correctly at checkout.
  • Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp or email and we will resend it.
